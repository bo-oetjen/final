FAQ

Straight answers before you reach out.

What it costs, how long it takes, and what we need from you. If your question isn't here, ask us on the contact page.

Questions we hear most.

How long does an engagement take?

Sprints run 1 - 2 weeks; Build-Outs typically take 3 - 6 weeks depending on scope. The Readiness Audit itself takes half a day.

What happens after the Readiness Audit?

You get a written summary and a fixed quote for next steps; no obligation to continue.

What if it doesn't work for my business?

You'll know that for $750, not after a long, expensive contract. That's the point of starting there.

How much does this cost?

Every engagement has a flat fee that you see before we start. The Readiness Audit is $750, an Automation Sprint is $1,500 - $3,000, a Core Build-Out is $4,000 - $9,000, and a Fractional Advisor is $500 - $1,500 per month. The price doesn't change when you add employees.

How much of my time will this take?

Expect about twenty minutes for the first conversation, then a half day for the Readiness Audit. During a Sprint or Build-Out, plan on a short weekly check-in and one training session for your team. We do the rest.

Do I need technical staff to use what you build?

No. We build around the tools you already use and train your team in person, so nobody needs an IT background to run it.

Is my business data safe?

We only access what each project needs, and we put what we touch, and how, in writing before we start. Your data stays yours, and we'll sign your confidentiality agreement if you have one.

Do you work in person or remotely?

In person. Every engagement includes visits to your business throughout the Cincinnati region, with remote check-ins in between.

Will you push me toward certain software?

No. Our advice is vendor-neutral, and we don't take commissions from any tool we recommend. If the right answer is a tool you already own, we'll say so.

Still have a question?

The first conversation costs nothing but about twenty minutes.
