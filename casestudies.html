Illustrative Case Studies

Three small businesses, three flat fees.

Illustrative example

Family Dental Practice

8 staff · Automation Sprint · $2,400 flat fee

The problem

The front desk spent hours each day on phone tag, confirming appointments and rescheduling, and calls that came in after hours went unanswered.

What we built

Automated booking, reminders, and rescheduling, plus an after-hours assistant that answers routine questions and routes the rest.

The result

Missed calls dropped by about 70%, and no-shows fell from 14% to 6% within 60 days.

Illustrative example

Heating & Plumbing Company

15 staff · Core Build-Out · $6,500 flat fee

The problem

The owner wrote every quote at night from handwritten site notes. Quotes took two days to reach customers, and faster competitors won the job.

What we built

Quote and proposal automation, automatic invoice processing, and hands-on training so the whole office could run it.

The result

Quotes went out the same day, and the office recovered about 9 hours a week.
