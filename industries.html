Industries

Built for the businesses AI usually skips.

Lean teams, no IT department, and too many hats. Here is where small businesses in the Cincinnati region lose the most time, and how we fix it.

Industries We Serve

Eight industries, one flat-fee approach.

Medical & Dental Offices

The pain point

The front desk loses hours to phone tag, appointment confirmations, and no-shows, and after-hours calls go unanswered.

The fix

Automated booking, reminders, and rescheduling, plus an after-hours assistant that answers routine questions and routes the rest.

Legal & Accounting Firms

The pain point

Staff keep answering the same client questions, and client documents arrive as a mix of PDFs, scans, and phone photos.

The fix

Document processing that reads and sorts incoming files, and a knowledge assistant trained on your own policies and deadlines.

Restaurants

The pain point

Phone calls for reservations, hours, and menu questions pull staff away from guests during the rush.

The fix

Inquiry response and booking automation, so routine calls are handled and your team stays on the floor.

Home Services & Trades

The pain point

The owner writes quotes at night from handwritten notes, and slow quotes lose jobs to faster competitors.

The fix

Quote and proposal automation from details you already have, plus automatic invoice processing.

Nonprofits

The pain point

A small staff is stretched across donor questions, volunteer scheduling, and grant paperwork.

The fix

Scheduling automation, a knowledge assistant for volunteers and staff, and vendor-neutral advice so you don't overpay for software.

Retail

The pain point

The same customer questions come in all day, and supplier invoices are entered by hand.

The fix

Inquiry response for common questions, and invoice processing that reads and enters supplier bills for you.

Property Management

The pain point

Tenant calls, maintenance requests, and lease paperwork arrive from every direction and get handled one at a time.

The fix

Inquiry routing, automated scheduling for maintenance visits, and document processing for leases and forms.

Manufacturing

The pain point

Paper work orders, supplier invoices, and quote requests slow down a lean office.

The fix

Document processing for orders and invoices, faster quotes, and a knowledge assistant for your procedures.
