About

Hi, I'm Bo.

I didn't set out to become the person my team calls when something needs an AI answer - it just happened.

As a Principal Technical Account Manager, and now my team's AI subject matter expert, I spend my days helping businesses get more out of the tools they already have.

The more of that work I did, the more one thing bothered me: the businesses that could benefit most from AI - the ones running lean, wearing ten hats, without an IT department down the hall - are exactly the ones priced out of it.

Etch AI Consulting exists to close that gap - bringing the same automation big companies take for granted to the small and mid-size businesses that make up most of the Cincinnati region, without the per-seat price tag.
